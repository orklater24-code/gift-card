Method

No black box.

01 — Tags, not profiles

Your answers add weight to plain tags: opinions, apps, gaming, shopping, quick, long, max. An offer scores by summing the weights of the tags it carries. That is the entire algorithm.

02 — Ties break on value

Equal scores are separated by reward-per-minute — the reward midpoint divided by the longest stated time — so the shortlist favours the offer that respects your hour.

03 — Estimates, not promises

The fit bar is relative to the best-scoring offer for your answers. Reward figures are Cardora estimates from partner listings; the partner's own page states the real terms.
