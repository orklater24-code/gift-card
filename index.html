About Us

About RewardHub

Our Mission

RewardHub is an online platform that connects consumers with promotional offers and market research opportunities from advertising partners across the United States, Canada, and United Kingdom.

We work with a network of trusted advertising partners to bring you promotional opportunities. Our platform serves as a directory, and all offers are provided and fulfilled by third-party partners under their own terms and conditions.

By participating in offers listed on our platform, users engage directly with our advertising partners. All reward fulfillment, verification, and eligibility requirements are determined solely by the respective partners.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is RewardHub?
RewardHub is a directory platform that lists promotional offers from various advertising partners. We connect consumers with third-party offers but do not directly fulfill or provide the rewards ourselves.
How do the offers work?
When you click on an offer, you will be redirected to our advertising partner's website. Each partner has their own terms, requirements, and reward structure. Please read the details carefully before participating.
Who can participate?
Our platform is available for residents of the United States, Canada, and United Kingdom who are 18 years or older. Specific offers may have additional eligibility requirements set by the advertising partner.
Is participation free?
Browsing our directory is free. However, individual offers may have their own terms and requirements. Please review each offer's terms and conditions before participating.
How is my data protected?
Our website uses SSL encryption. When you click on partner offers, you will interact with third-party sites that have their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing partner privacy policies before providing any information.
