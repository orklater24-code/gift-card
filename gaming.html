Transparency

What kills a gaming offer.

Gaming and app offers have more rejection reasons than surveys do. Nearly all of them are avoidable, and none of them are Cardora's call — the partner validates the activity.

Usually pays when…

  • ✓You install on a real, personal device and open the app through the partner's own link.
  • ✓You reach the exact milestone the partner names — level, session length or spend.
  • ✓You stay on one device, one browser, one account for the whole run.
  • ✓The account is new to that partner and your country is supported.

Usually voids it when…

  • ✕You run an emulator, rooted device or virtual phone — most partners detect and reject these.
  • ✕You already had the app installed before starting the offer.
  • ✕You stop one step short of the milestone, or uninstall before it logs.
  • ✕An in-app purchase is refunded, or a VPN masks your real country.
