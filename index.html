The mechanics

Three steps, no fourth one hiding.

01

Pick an offer

Read the reward range and the time cost on the card first. That's the whole point of this page.

Nothing is tracked until you click.

02

Finish the partner's task

You leave Cardora and land on the partner's site. You follow their steps — survey, install, milestone or purchase.

Their terms govern, not ours. Read them before you start.

03

Partner reviews & releases

The partner checks the activity against their rules. If it passes, they release the reward on their own timeline.

Incomplete or ineligible activity is rejected. That is normal, not a bug.

Transparency

What decides whether you get paid.

Most reward sites never say this. It's the single most useful thing you can know before spending twenty minutes on an offer.

Usually pays when…

  • ✓You complete the offer on one device and one browser, start to finish.
  • ✓You reach the exact milestone the partner names — not one step short of it.
  • ✓Your details match the account you signed up with.
  • ✓You're inside the partner's country and age rules.

Usually voids it when…

  • ✕Ad blockers, private windows or VPNs are on during the offer.
  • ✕You've completed the same offer before on that account.
  • ✕The offer needed a purchase and it was refunded.
  • ✕Multiple accounts from one household or IP.
